BE PREPARED

A little preparation
goes a long way.

Families may be asked to provide documents such as:

  • Student's birth certificate
  • Previous school reports, if applicable
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Parent or guardian contact details
  • Transfer certificate, where applicable

These are examples only. Please confirm the exact requirements and eligibility with the school.
